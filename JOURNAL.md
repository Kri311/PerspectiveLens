# PerspectiveLens Journal

This markdown is a continuous write-up which I'll maintain as I go with the flow of this project.

This project is made under the guidance and mentorship of Ayshwarya Kurup as a semester project in the Text Analytics subject.

## History

Perspective is knowledge, and it plays a major role in our lives—from perceiving what an event, person, or technology represents. Yet, these perspectives can be easily manipulated by authors of news channels through false accusations, exaggeration, or suppression of facts. And yet, we often believe those STORIES.

I got the idea for this topic from a YouTube channel @Horses, where he promoted "Ground News" as a "Veritas" form of news. After doing my research, I decided to try building something similar but specialized for the Tamil Nadu media landscape. That's how **PerspectiveLens** was born—an event-centric Tamil news intelligence platform designed to computationally detect differences in media framing, facts, and coverage blindspots.
